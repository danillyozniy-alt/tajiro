Frequently asked questions

Is it really free to start? +
Yes. Your store, your products and your Growth Manager are there from day one. After the 14-day trial it is AED 149 a month – no upfront cost to begin.
Do I need any technical skills? +
None. If you can fill a form and click a button, you can run this. We handle the website, hosting, checkout and delivery.
What will I sell? +
AI-powered digital tools – visa guides, gratuity calculators, school roadmaps, Ramadan planners. You can also build your own with us. Downloadable products your customers use themselves. No physical goods.
How do the margins work? +
You keep 50–70% of each sale on catalog products, and more on products you create yourself. What you earn depends on your effort, your audience and how you promote your store – we do not promise specific numbers.
How are products delivered? +
Instantly and automatically. The moment a customer buys, they get access. The whole process is hands-free.
What does a Growth Manager do? +
A real person who helps you start, answers questions, reviews your store and gives tailored advice by chat and scheduled calls.
