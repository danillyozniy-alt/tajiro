OUR STORY

From idea to global platform

Tajiro was built with one belief: starting an online business shouldn’t require a warehouse, technical skills, or a big budget. It should be simple, automated, and designed to give you back your most valuable resource – time.

We are an ecommerce platform built exclusively for solopreneurs. We provide everything you need to launch, run, and grow a successful online venture: a ready-made store, digital products to sell, and automated marketing tools – all working together on autopilot.
